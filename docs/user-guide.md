# EcoRoute User Guide & Walkthrough

Welcome to EcoRoute — Official Government of India standard AI-powered e-waste management portal.

## Getting Started

### 1. Citizen Portal
- **Listing Scrap Items**: Navigate to `Upload E-Waste`, capture a photo using live camera scan or upload an image. AI Vision automatically identifies the item, category, and estimated valuation price.
- **Redeeming Green Coins**: Navigate to `Rewards & Points` to convert green coins into direct **Cash (UPI/GPay)**, **Electricity (EB) Bill Discounts**, or **Water Bill Waivers**.

### 2. Recycler / Buyer Portal
- **Browsing Scrap Marketplace**: Navigate to `/buyer` to browse verified scrap listings.
- **Purchasing & Scheduling Pickup**: Click `Buy / Intimate Pickup & Cash`. Select your pickup collection date, time slot, and payment method (Cash on Pickup, live scannable UPI QR code, or Credit/Debit Card).
- **Official Bill Receipt & Navigation**: Download the CPCB compliant purchase token and launch Google Maps directions directly to the seller's pickup location.

### 3. Government Officer & Admin Portal
- **Priority Queue Management**: View hazardous e-waste listings flagged for priority processing (e.g. Lead-acid batteries, CRT glass monitors).
- **Compliance Certification**: Monitor CPCB recycling certificates issued to verified recyclers.
